tapedup-payment-rules: one-time setup

Each panel below activates one Shopify Function from this app. You only need to run each once — after that, the rule lives in Shopify and this page is done for it. Safe to reload or come back later; each panel checks whether it's already set up before offering to create anything.

Hide trade-account payment method

Hides "Trade Account – Invoice / Net Terms" from checkout for everyone except customers tagged "b2b".

B2B trade pricing

    
Applies each product's custom.wholesale_price metafield (or a flat fallback %) as an automatic discount for customers tagged "b2b" — replaces creating a matching discount by hand per product. Set to combine with your other automatic discounts (e.g. Buy 4 get 1 free) so dealers get both at once.

B2B shipping: hide other rates

    
Matches delivery options by name: any rate with "B2B" in its title is shown only to customers tagged "b2b" and hidden from everyone else; every other rate is shown only to regular customers and hidden from dealers. Create at least one shipping rate with "B2B" in its name (e.g. "B2B Standard Shipping") for dealers to have anything to check out with — optionally add a second one named e.g. "B2B Priority (24hr)" for a paid fast-shipping upgrade.

B2B trade shipping (free above threshold / flat rate below)

    
For customers tagged "b2b", discounts any delivery option with "B2B" in its title down to free (at/above the threshold) or a flat rate (below it) — except one also named "Priority", which instead gets discounted down to a fixed price regardless of order value.

Starter order discount

    
Takes each customer's starter-order discount off their whole order at checkout. You set who gets what in the panel below. Stacks on top of B2B trade pricing and shipping.

Starter orders

    
Give a specific customer a one-time starter order. The products you pick go into their cart automatically the next time they visit the site logged in, a banner shows the offer until they order, and the discount comes off their whole order at checkout. It switches itself off once they've placed their next order.

    
They need to be logged in to their account for the site and checkout to recognise them.
